Magic & Superpower Hackathon

A build-first hackathon where early stage builders turn magical artifacts, superpowers, sci-fi gadgets, AI sidekicks, and fictional interfaces into working prototypes.

✨ Explore Prompts

Why — Builder Confidence

This event is designed for builder confidence and creative exploration. It provides a welcoming, low-pressure environment where participants can work on bringing imaginative ideas to life.

Early stage builders do not need to solve a serious problem in order to learn how to build. The intended outcome is foundational.

"Early stage builders should leave thinking, 'I can make weird ideas real.'"

Who — The Builders

This build festival is designed for three kinds of participants:

  • Curious Students: Eager to learn, experiment, and write their first lines of code.
  • Early Builders: Makers turning imaginative ideas and fiction into working prototypes.
  • Enthusiastic Learners: Anyone interested in technology, ready to tinker and learn something new.

Whether you're building a sci-fi gadget or coding an enchanted UI, this is your playground.

Rules of Enchantment

To prioritize playful experimentation and hands-on learning:

  • Creative Freedom: Build imaginative, fun, or theatrical prototypes without needing a commercial business case.
  • Hands-On Execution: Prioritize working software, interactive devices, and real proof of concept.
  • Memorable Demos: Aim to create a delightful experience that demonstrates your craft.

The standout projects are those that turn imaginative concepts into interactive, working demos.

What You Can Build

The theme is speculative fiction, including magic, science fiction, fantasy, superheroes, futuristic interfaces, and impossible-feeling machines.

Take something that feels like it belongs in fiction and make a working prototype. Teams can build a talking painting that responds to visitors, a fake Jarvis that gives dramatic assistance, a spellbook interface for triggering automations, or a wearable that gives silly "powers" based on motion.

🪄 Spells & Magic 🛡️ Superpower Gadgets 🤖 Sci-Fi & AI Sidekicks 🌀 Impossible Machines

Safety is a Hard Boundary

The theme includes magic, superpowers, and fictional devices, but every build must be safe for participants, audiences, and venues.

We encourage translating dangerous fictional concepts into safe physical proxies—using lights, sound effects, motion sensors, haptics, and software interfaces to create the feeling of magic without real-world risk.

The Build Festival Format

This event is structured as a high-energy build festival. We start with a showcase of playful builds, form teams around shared moments of wonder, and dedicate time to building alongside mentors, culminating in a theatrical live showcase.

  1. 1 Inspire (Playful showcase)
  2. 2 Choose (Fictional prompts)
  3. 3 Build (Hands-on creation)
  4. 4 Demo (Live theatrical proof)

Judging Wonder Before Utility

Evaluation criteria focus directly on creative execution, rewarding wonder, craft, demo quality, and learning. Practical utility is welcome, but secondary to the magic of making something real.
