# Fiction Prompts for Magic & Superpower Hackathon

This document collects fiction-grounded prompts for the hackathon. Each prompt starts from a familiar fictional character, object, power, or plot device, then translates it into a safe prototype direction.

The goal is not to recreate fiction exactly. The goal is to build a working version of the feeling: a magical interaction, a futuristic interface, a dramatic gadget, an impossible object, or a playful superpower.

## Prompt Format

Each prompt should answer three questions:

1. What fictional idea is this inspired by?
2. What is the buildable version?
3. What is the safe demo moment?

## Magic and Fantasy

### Talking Portrait

Inspired by: The moving and talking portraits in Harry Potter.

Buildable version: A framed screen or web page that listens, responds in character, and reacts to the room using voice, camera, or sensor input.

Safe demo moment: A visitor asks the portrait for advice and receives an overdramatic response.

### Wand Interface

Inspired by: Spell casting in Harry Potter and other fantasy stories.

Buildable version: A gesture-controlled wand that triggers harmless actions such as changing slides, switching lights, playing sounds, or controlling a website.

Safe demo moment: A participant performs a spell gesture and the room responds.

### Enchanted Map

Inspired by: Magical maps that reveal hidden people, places, or paths.

Buildable version: A map interface that changes based on location, QR scans, Bluetooth signals, or manual check-ins.

Safe demo moment: The map reveals where team members, mentors, or fictional missions are located.

### Magic Mirror

Inspired by: Mirrors that speak, judge, or reveal hidden truths in fairy tales and fantasy stories.

Buildable version: A screen-and-camera setup that reacts to facial expression, outfit color, voice, or posture.

Safe demo moment: The mirror gives a dramatic status report when someone stands in front of it.

## Superheroes and Powers

### Web-Slinger Prop

Inspired by: Spider-Man's web shooters.

Buildable version: A completely non-hazardous wrist prop that uses lights, sound, haptics, or a soft visual effect instead of any real projectile.

Safe demo moment: A wrist gesture triggers a web animation, sound cue, or harmless string-light effect.

### Utility Suit Dashboard

Inspired by: Iron Man's suit interface.

Buildable version: A wearable or desktop dashboard that shows fake suit status, sensor readings, voice commands, alerts, and dramatic system messages.

Safe demo moment: The dashboard reacts to voice commands and announces a fictional mission status.

### Detective Vision

Inspired by: Batman-style tactical analysis and detective overlays.

Buildable version: A camera or web app that labels ordinary objects as if they are clues, threats, tools, or mission items.

Safe demo moment: The system scans a desk and creates a dramatic investigation report.

### Power Gesture System

Inspired by: Superheroes who activate powers through motion or posture.

Buildable version: A phone, webcam, accelerometer, or wearable sensor that maps gestures to light, sound, animation, or browser actions.

Safe demo moment: A pose or movement triggers a visible "power" effect.

## More Worlds and Powers

These prompts widen the starting points with concrete capabilities: changing reality, building intelligent tools, sensing the world, checking in with people, and creating responsive hardware.

### Reality Rewrite Console

Inspired by: Wanda's reality-altering powers.

Buildable version: An app that takes a simple scene, story, or room layout and generates several safe alternate versions using text, images, sound, or projected light.

Safe demo moment: A participant changes one detail and the system transforms the whole scene in front of the audience.

### Suit Copilot

Inspired by: Ironheart's self-built armor and AI assistant.

Buildable version: A wearable or desktop copilot that reads sensors, answers voice questions, reports system status, and suggests the next build step.

Safe demo moment: A participant asks the copilot for a status report and the suit responds using lights, a screen, or audio.

### Krypton Check-In

Inspired by: Supergirl's enhanced senses and her concern for people at a distance.

Buildable version: An opt-in app for checking how teammates are doing through a simple status, mood, or “need help” signal—without tracking location or reading private messages.

Safe demo moment: A team member taps a status button and the shared mission board updates with a clear, human-readable signal.

### Enhanced Senses Viewer

Inspired by: Supergirl's enhanced vision and hearing.

Buildable version: A camera-and-microphone tool that magnifies, color-codes, captions, or filters ordinary environmental signals for a safe demonstration.

Safe demo moment: The viewer reveals a hidden color, quiet sound, or small visual detail and explains the result in text.

### Spell Command Gauntlet

Inspired by: Wanda's energy control and gesture-based magic.

Buildable version: A glove, wristband, or handheld controller with an accelerometer, LED ring, and haptic motor that maps gestures to software or room effects.

Safe demo moment: A hand movement triggers a glowing pattern, vibration, and a harmless change on screen.

### Armor Diagnostics Kit

Inspired by: Ironheart's powered armor and engineering mindset.

Buildable version: A safe wearable frame or backpack with temperature, light, motion, and battery sensors that displays live diagnostics on a small screen.

Safe demo moment: The builder moves, covers a light sensor, or warms the device slightly and the armor dashboard reacts.

### Rescue Beacon

Inspired by: Cosmic heroes coordinating help across long distances.

Buildable version: A tabletop hardware beacon with buttons, LEDs, a buzzer, and a local wireless message that lets a team request help or announce a mission state.

Safe demo moment: Pressing the beacon changes the signal from “available” to “need a hand,” and the nearby team display responds.

## Science Fiction and AI

### Personal AI Sidekick

Inspired by: Jarvis-like assistants in science fiction.

Buildable version: A voice or chat assistant that performs small, theatrical tasks such as generating missions, narrating actions, or controlling simple devices.

Safe demo moment: The AI gives the team a mission briefing and triggers one harmless automation.

### Hologram Console

Inspired by: Futuristic control rooms and sci-fi command interfaces.

Buildable version: A projected, AR, or web-based interface that makes ordinary controls feel like a starship console.

Safe demo moment: The team controls lights, sounds, or animations through a dramatic command interface.

### Universal Translator

Inspired by: Real-time translators in Star Trek and other science fiction.

Buildable version: A speech-to-text and translation demo that converts short phrases into another language or into a fictional tone.

Safe demo moment: Someone speaks normally and the system responds as a space diplomat, wizard, or mission computer.

### Memory Replay Device

Inspired by: Fictional devices that replay memories, dreams, or past events.

Buildable version: A photo, audio, or text interface that turns uploaded moments into an interactive timeline, narrated scene, or visual reconstruction.

Safe demo moment: A small set of demo inputs becomes a dramatic memory playback.

## Objects and Worlds

### Pocket Companion

Inspired by: Doraemon-style gadgets and helpful fictional companions.

Buildable version: A small app, chatbot, or hardware box that offers absurd tools for ordinary situations.

Safe demo moment: A participant asks for help with a normal task and receives an exaggerated gadget suggestion.

### Portal Door

Inspired by: Portals and magical doorways in fantasy and science fiction.

Buildable version: A door, screen, or camera installation that changes scenes, sounds, or instructions when someone approaches.

Safe demo moment: Walking through a marked space triggers a new fictional world state.

### Mission Generator

Inspired by: Quest systems in games, superhero teams, and fantasy adventures.

Buildable version: A generator that turns ordinary campus activities into quests with roles, constraints, and dramatic stakes.

Safe demo moment: The system assigns a team a funny mission based on random inputs.

### Overengineered Alarm

Inspired by: Villain lairs, secret bases, and dramatic security systems.

Buildable version: A harmless alarm system with sensors, lights, voice alerts, and exaggerated escalation states.

Safe demo moment: Someone opens a box or crosses a line and the system launches a theatrical alert sequence.

## Prompt Selection Principle

Good prompts should be familiar enough to spark ideas, but open enough that teams can interpret them differently.

The best prompt is not "build the exact thing from the movie." The best prompt is "build a safe prototype that creates the same kind of moment."
